ORVIN Privacy Policy ORVIN is a private automation tool operated by a single creator to upload and schedule videos to the creator's own YouTube channel. Data accessed: Using the YouTube API, the tool uploads videos and sets their metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, schedule) on the creator's own channel, and may read that channel's own video statistics. Users: The tool has no users other than the channel owner. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people. Storage: Authorization tokens are stored securely in the creator's private n8n account and are used only for the actions above. Revoking access: Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms. Contact: watchorvin@gmail.com
